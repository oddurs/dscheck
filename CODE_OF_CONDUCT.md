# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

Concretely: harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, and unwelcome attention
have no place here. Neither does bad-faith argument — repeating a point louder, litigating
a settled decision, or demanding unpaid labour from maintainers.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other
project space.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to oddurs@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this standard, and may
block accounts for repeated or severe violations. Enforcement decisions are explained to
the reporter and, where appropriate, to the person involved.

## Scope

This project takes the same view of conduct that it takes of findings: judgment is
stated plainly, applied consistently, and open to being shown wrong.
